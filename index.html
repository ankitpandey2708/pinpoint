§ 01 · The problem

Feedback shouldn’t live somewhere else

•

Scattered feedback

By the time a freelancer reads it, they’re guessing what “that” refers to.

•

Slow back-and-forth

Ambiguous notes mean another round of “wait, which section did you mean?”

•

Small fixes, big context-switch

Even clear feedback still costs a full context-switch to turn into an actual change.

•

No single source of truth

Feedback lives across five different tools, so nobody’s sure which version is current.

§ 02 · How it works

From comment to fix, automatically

Three steps from “here’s what’s wrong” to a fix ready for review.

Step 01

Pin it

Click directly on the live page and leave a comment right where it applies.

Step 02

AI sorts it

Every comment is triaged automatically by effort.

Low Mid High
Step 03

Low-effort → PR

An agent drafts the fix as a pull request. A human always reviews before it merges.
