# Pin Point

**Feedback, pinned right where it happens.**

## The problem

Feedback on a landing page almost never lives on the landing page. It shows up as a screenshot in Slack with a red arrow, an email that says "can you move that up a bit?", or a comment three scroll-lengths deep in a shared doc. By the time a freelancer reads it, they're guessing what "that" even refers to.

And even when feedback *is* clear, someone still has to translate it into an actual change — find the file, make the edit, open a PR. For small stuff, that context-switch often costs more time than the fix itself.

## How it works

1. **Pin it.** Clients and internal teams leave comments directly on the live page — click the spot, say what's wrong. No screenshots, no guessing what "that" means.
2. **AI sorts it.** Every comment gets triaged automatically as low, mid, or high effort.
3. **Low-effort fixes become PRs.** For the small stuff, an AI agent drafts the change as a pull request. A human reviews before anything merges.

Mid and high effort feedback stays exactly what it should be — a clear, contextual note for the freelancer to pick up, no guessing required.

## Who it's for

**Clients & internal teams**
Leave feedback where you see it, in plain language. No new tool to learn, no ticket format to follow.

**Freelancers**
Get feedback that's unambiguous and located exactly where it applies. Spend review time on the changes that need judgment — let the easy ones show up as a PR ready to check.

## Why it matters

The gap between "here's what's wrong" and "here's the fix" is where most freelance feedback cycles lose time — not because the feedback is hard to give, but because it's disconnected from the thing it's about, and the small fixes still take a full context-switch to execute. Pin Point closes that gap: feedback stays attached to the page it's about, and the easy fixes stop waiting on someone's afternoon.
